Person-Centred Point of Service System (PCPOSS) (PCPOSS) candidate

Person-centred point of service system (PCPOSS) are digital systems that facilitate the provision and delivery of health services to individuals (i.e. persons, clients, patients, health service users) at the point of service or point of care. This includes software capabilities and embedded health interoperability standards that enable health workers to access, record and update individuals’ health information. PCPOSS also includes software capabilities and embedded health interoperability standards that enable screening, managing, treating, and/or communicating with, individuals. PCPOSS encompass various services and application types, including community-based information systems, decision support systems, electronic medical (or health) record systems and personal health records (3).

Also called: PCPOSS, Person-Centred Point of Service Systems (PCPOSS), Person-centred point of service system (PCPOSS)

Handbook definition, quoted. Open: not adopted, nothing chosen between alternatives. Section 1.1 and the Glossary note that PCPOSS encompasses 4 service/application types (community-based information systems, decision support systems, electronic medical/health record systems, and personal health records), while Annex 1 maps PCPOSS functional requirements across 13 WHO Classification of Digital Health Interventions (DHIs): 1.1.1, 1.4.1, 2.1.1, 2.2.1, 2.2.2, 2.2.4, 2.3.1, 2.3.2, 2.4.3, 2.7.1, 2.7.2, 2.8.1, and 2.9.1. Section 1.1 and Annex 1 synthesis contrasting the 4 application types with the 13 DHIs delivered by PCPOSS. Alternative definition (who-iris/library/9789240093362-eng/sections/sec-004-11-person-centred-point-of-service-systems.md): "Person-centred point of service systems (PCPOSS) encompass four primary application types (community-based information systems, decision support systems, electronic medical/health record systems, and personal health records) and operationalize 13 WHO Digital Health Interventions (DHIs) across Annex 1."
