Business Process candidate

A set of activities and tasks performed in coordination to achieve a specified business objective, or to deliver value in a specific way to a health service user. In the context of digitizing care pathways, this could include “health service user registration” and “referrals”, for example (see section 3.3).

Also called: Business process

Handbook definition, quoted. Open: not adopted, nothing chosen between alternatives.
