Undertake design and adaptation (Ch 4, 4.1-4.5)

Process_DesignAndAdaptation · advisory · 11 step(s)

Select the software and vendor, validate functional requirements with stakeholders, iteratively design and test wireframes, mock-ups and clickable prototypes with end-users, specify the system and interoperability architecture, and build and QA-test the beta PCPOSS.

lane role what it does here
Business analyst & implementing team pers:business-analyst Persona blocks pers:business-analyst and pers:implementing-team. Software engineer / technology partner pers:software-engineer Persona blocks pers:software-engineer and pers:technology-partner. Leads technical build, product documentation and the seven-part quality assurance test suite (installation, stress, security, graphic UI, integration, QA and accessibility testing, 4.5).

step lane skill / sub-process what it does
4.1 Finalize software & vendor selection (bespoke, COTS or reference software)
Task_4_1_SelectSoftwareVendor		 Business analyst & implementing team l2-dependency-choice Section 4.1 (prose-sec-020, pages 50-53, Table 2): Conduct landscape analysis and scorecard evaluation across bespoke software, commercial/open-source COTS, digital public goods and SMART Guidelines L4 reference software.
4.2 Validate functional requirements in stakeholder workshops & design test use cases (4.3.2)
Task_4_2_ValidateRequirements		 Business analyst & implementing team content-review Section 4.2 (prose-sec-021, page 53) and Section 4.3.2 Step 1 (prose-sec-022, page 55): Validate and confirm functional requirements with key stakeholders before build, and design testing use cases from Section 3.2 use cases and user stories.
4.3.1 Draw low-fidelity sketches and wireframes
Task_4_3_Wireframe		 Designer wireframe-design-review Section 4.3.1 and Fig. 15 (prose-sec-022, pages 53-55): Create low-fidelity paper sketches and non-clickable wireframes depicting overall layout and data-entry flow.
4.3.2 Test wireframe structure & data-entry flow with end-users
Task_4_3_TestWireframe		 Targeted health worker occupational groups & end-users wireframe-design-review Section 4.3.1-4.3.2 and Fig. 15 (prose-sec-022, pages 54-56): Invite end-users to walk through scenarios on the wireframes to validate overall structure and information organization.
4.3.1 Create moderate-fidelity visual mock-ups
Task_4_3_Mockup		 Designer wireframe-design-review Section 4.3.1 and Fig. 15 (prose-sec-022, pages 54-55): Create visual screen mock-ups with colour schemes, flags/notifications, language tone and scheduling timeframes.
4.3.2 Test mock-ups (look, feel & language) with end-users
Task_4_3_TestMockup		 Targeted health worker occupational groups & end-users wireframe-design-review Section 4.3.2 (prose-sec-022, pages 55-56): Test multiple mock-up options with end-users and document structured feedback (things liked, confusing features, improvements).
4.3.1 Build higher-fidelity clickable prototype
Task_4_3_Prototype		 Designer wireframe-design-review Section 4.3.1 and Fig. 15 (prose-sec-022, pages 54-55): Build an interactive, clickable prototype where buttons navigate across screens and simulate clinical care pathways.
4.3.2 Validate clickable prototype via end-user role play
Task_4_3_TestPrototype		 Targeted health worker occupational groups & end-users wireframe-design-review Section 4.3.2 (prose-sec-022, pages 55-56): End-users experiment with the clickable prototype (‘Show, don’t tell’) in role-play scenarios; feedback is documented and vetted by stakeholders to prevent scope creep.
4.4 Design system architecture & interoperability standards (FHIR, HIE)
Task_4_4_Architecture		 Solutions architect & health informatician archimate-models Section 4.4 (prose-sec-023, pages 56-60, Table 3, Figs. 16-18) and Annex 14 (prose-sec-046): Define business, data, application and technology architecture viewpoints (4.4.1), foundational/structural/semantic/organizational interoperability (4.4.2), and health data, terminology and HL7 FHIR exchange standards (4.4.3). Links to smart-ra PCPOSS, CDS, SHR and Interoperability views.
4.5 Establish test plan, build beta PCPOSS & product documentation
Task_4_5_BuildAndDocument		 Software engineer / technology partner l4-application-build Section 4.5 (prose-sec-024, pages 60-61): Establish comprehensive test plan once requirements are finalized, build or adapt the PCPOSS beta version in sprints, and author product documentation.
4.5 Execute installation, stress, security, UI, integration, QA & accessibility tests
Task_4_5_RunQaTests		 Software engineer / technology partner content-test Section 4.5 (prose-sec-024, page 61): Technology partner runs installation testing, stress testing, security testing, graphic UI testing, integration testing, quality assurance testing and accessibility testing.
