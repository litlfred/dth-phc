# Donors, development partners and implementing partners

International and domestic funding agencies, multilateral organizations, technical assistance partners and non-governmental implementing partners named in the handbook's target audience (section 1.3, `sec-006`) and Phase 1 / Phase 4 involvement panels (`sec-011`, `sec-029`):

- **Role in the handbook:** Align investments with the national digital health strategy and DIIG phases rather than funding fragmented vertical tools (2.3, `sec-009`), participate in early stakeholder alignment (3.1), and co-finance sustainable long-term operations, infrastructure and capacity building for national scale-up (6.1, `sec-029`).
