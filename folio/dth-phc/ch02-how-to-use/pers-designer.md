# Designer

A core member of the PCPOSS implementing team (section 2.2) responsible for human-centred interface design and prototyping:

- **Role definition (section 2.2):** *"Someone who focuses on how the software looks and feels to the user, making sure it is easy to understand and navigate."*
- **Phase 2 — Design and adaptation (Chapter 4, `sec-020`, `sec-022`):** Designs the three progressive prototype stages in section 4.3 — low-fidelity wireframes, visual mock-ups, and interactive clickable prototypes — iterating each stage based on end-user usability testing with health workers and health service users.
