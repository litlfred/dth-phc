# Public health authority / Ministry of Health (MoH)

The primary target audience and governing authority of the handbook (section 1.3, `sec-006`; Phase 1–4 panels): national and subnational decision-makers, digital health directors and PHC programme managers within the Ministry of Health or public health agency:

- **Governance and stewardship:** Aligns the PCPOSS initiative with the national digital health strategy and enterprise architecture (2.3), charters the multidisciplinary implementing team (2.2), approves requirement priorities (`requirement-priority.dmn`, 3.8), establishes data-protection, device-management and clinical governance policies before roll-out (5.0, `sec-025`), and governs the scale-up readiness decision (`ready-to-scale-up.dmn`, 6.1–6.2).
