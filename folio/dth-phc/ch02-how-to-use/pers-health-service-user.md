# Health service user (patient / client)

Any person receiving or seeking primary health care services, as well as their family members or caregivers, and the direct user of Personal Health Record (PHR) and targeted client communication capabilities:

- **Glossary definition (`sec-003`):** *"A person who uses or benefits from health services, often referred to as a patient or client."*
- **Role in the handbook:** Named in the Phase 1 (`sec-011`), Phase 2 (`sec-020`) and Phase 3 (`sec-025`) panels; participates in user research (3.2) and prototype usability testing (4.3), and appears as the `Client` swimlane in the reproduced WHO ANC DAK Workflow A registration business process (Figs. 10 & 11, section 3.3.2).
