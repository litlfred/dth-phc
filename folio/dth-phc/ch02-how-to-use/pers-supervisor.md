# Supervisor / health facility manager

Health facility in-charges, clinical supervisors and district PHC supervisors who oversee service delivery, data quality, staff training and change management at the facility level:

- **Role in the handbook:** Named in the Phase 1 (`sec-011`), Phase 3 (`sec-025`) and Phase 4 (`sec-029`) panels; participates in requirements elicitation (3.1–3.2), oversees facility-level training and UAT (5.1–5.2), leads the staged phase-out of legacy paper registers and paper backup SOPs (6.4, `sec-032`), and champions change management and an electronic data-use culture (6.5, `sec-033`).
