# Business analyst

A core member of the PCPOSS implementing team (section 2.2) who translates health service delivery needs, clinical guidelines and operational workflows into structured requirements:

- **Role summary (agent draft, not handbook text; for review):** Someone who helps bridge the gap between what users need and how the technology should work. They help translate health requirements into technical plans. The handbook does not define this role.
- **Phase 1 — Understanding user requirements (Chapter 3, `sec-011`):** Leads workflow mapping in BPMN (3.3), user persona and user story elicitation (3.2, 3.5), decision-support table authoring (3.6, Annex 11), scheduling logic definition (3.7, Annex 12), and requirements prioritization (3.8, Annex 13).
- **Phase 2 — Design and adaptation (Chapter 4, `sec-020`):** Validates functional requirements with end-users (4.2) and collaborates with designers and user researchers on wireframes, mock-ups and clickable prototypes (4.3).
