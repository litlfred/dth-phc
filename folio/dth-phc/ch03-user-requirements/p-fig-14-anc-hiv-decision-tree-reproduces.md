# Fig. 14. Illustrative ANC HIV-testing decision tree (reproduces WHO ANC DAK)

Section 3.6.1 (`sec-017`, PDF page 45, Fig. 14) reproduces an illustrative clinical decision tree for antenatal HIV testing from the **WHO Digital Adaptation Kit for Antenatal Care (ANC DAK, 2021)**, demonstrating how a visual branching decision tree is translated into the rows and input/output columns of an Annex 11 decision-support table ([`dt:annex-11-decision-support-table-template`](https://github.com/litlfred/dth-phc/blob/main/folio/dth-phc/annexes/dt-annex-11-decision-support-table-template.ts)).

- **Boundary rule (`reproduces` link, not re-extracted as PHC clinical logic):** The antenatal HIV testing algorithm belongs to the WHO ANC DAK (`ANC.DT.08`); `dth-phc` cites it solely as a worked example of tree-to-table translation in Step 3.6.
