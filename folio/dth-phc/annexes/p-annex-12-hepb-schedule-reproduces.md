# Annex 12 worked example: Hepatitis B birth-dose vaccination schedule (reproduces WHO Immunization DAK)

Annex 12 (`sec-044`, PDF page 99, and section 3.7 `sec-018`, PDF page 46) includes a worked example filling the Annex 12 scheduling-logic template ([`sched:annex-12-scheduling-logic-template`](sched-annex-12-scheduling-logic-template.ts)) with the **Hepatitis B birth-dose vaccination schedule** from the **WHO Digital Adaptation Kit for Immunizations (`smart-immunizations`)**.

- **Boundary rule (`reproduces` link, not re-extracted as PHC clinical logic):** Per `litlfred/dth-phc#1` §3b and §3d, the Hepatitis B birth-dose schedule is a reproduced example from the WHO Immunization DAK (`IMMZ.S.HepB`), whereas the scheduling-logic template columns are formalized in [`sched:annex-12-scheduling-logic-template`](sched-annex-12-scheduling-logic-template.ts).
