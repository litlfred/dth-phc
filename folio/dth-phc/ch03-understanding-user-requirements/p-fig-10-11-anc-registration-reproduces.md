# Figs. 10 and 11. Workflow A: ANC Registration business process and annotations (reproduces WHO ANC DAK)

Section 3.3.2 (`sec-014`, PDF pages 33–34, Figs. 10 and 11) reproduces an illustrative business-process diagram (**Fig. 10, Workflow A: Registration**) and its companion step-by-step annotation table (**Fig. 11**) from the **WHO Digital Adaptation Kit for Antenatal Care (ANC DAK, 2021)**.

- **Role in this handbook (`reproduces` link, not a PHC workflow):** Demonstrates how a country implementing team documents a clinical/operational workflow in BPMN with explicit swimlanes ([`pers:health-service-user`](../ch02-getting-started/pers-health-service-user.ts) / *Client*, [`pers:clerk`](../ch02-getting-started/pers-clerk.ts) / *Clerk*, and [`pers:health-worker`](../ch02-getting-started/pers-health-worker.ts) / *Health worker*), numbered activities, decision gateways and an accompanying annotation table (Annex 6).
- **Canonical upstream artefact:** WHO ANC DAK (`smart.who.int/anc`), Component 4 (*Business processes and workflows*), Workflow A (*Registration*).
