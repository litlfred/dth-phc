# Fig. 13. Illustrative HIV decision-support logic matrix (reproduces WHO HIV DAK)

Section 3.6.1 (`sec-017`, PDF pages 43–44, Fig. 13) reproduces an illustrative decision-support table from the **WHO Digital Adaptation Kit for HIV (HIV DAK)** to show how clinical guideline recommendations are formatted as a DMN-shaped decision table using the Annex 11 template ([`dt:annex-11-decision-support-table-template`](../annexes/dt-annex-11-decision-support-table-template.ts)).

- **Boundary rule (`reproduces` link, not re-extracted as PHC clinical logic):** The clinical rules in Fig. 13 belong to the WHO HIV DAK (`HIV.DT.*`), whereas the decision-table structure and hit-policy conventions belong to `dth-phc` (`sec-017` and Annex 11 `sec-043`).
