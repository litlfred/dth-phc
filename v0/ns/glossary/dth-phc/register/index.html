Label
Register (en)
Definition
In the context of health care, a list of health service users compiled for the purposes of tracking care, aggregating activities or health outcomes, or identifying health service users who need specific interventions. (en)
Scope note
Handbook definition, quoted. Open: not adopted, nothing chosen between alternatives. Rule register-is-not-registry: In PHC, a Register is a list of health service users compiled for tracking care, aggregating activities or health outcomes, or identifying health service users who need specific interventions (cohort tracking log). It must NOT be conflated with Registry (dpi-h-ra/registry), which is an HIE master index.
