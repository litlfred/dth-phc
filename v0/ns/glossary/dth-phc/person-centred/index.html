Definition
In the context of person-centred health services, person-centred refers to prioritizing the comprehensive needs of individuals (i.e. persons, clients, patients, health service users) at the centre of health systems. It involves actively engaging and empowering individuals to have a more active role in their own health and health-care decisions. It shifts the focus of health services away from specific diseases or interventions, towards holistic needs of individuals across the continuum of care and life course (21). (en)
