Interoperability (en)
Definition
Ability of different applications to access, exchange, integrate and use data in a coordinated and consistent manner through the use of shared application interfaces, value sets, concepts and standards, within and across organizational, regional and national boundaries, to provide timely and seamless portability of information and optimize health outcomes. (en)
Scope note
Handbook definition, quoted. Open: not adopted, nothing chosen between alternatives. Glossary entry (p. xi) is the primary definition; Section 4.4 body (HIMSS) and Section 4.4.2 (IEEE 1990) provide two additional definitions in the handbook. Section 4.4 body definition (p. 41), citing Healthcare Information and Management Systems Society (HIMSS). Alternative definition (who-iris/library/9789240093362-eng/sections/sec-023-44-design-system-architecture.md): "Interoperability ensures that all digital systems within the healthcare ecosystem work together harmoniously. It refers to the ability of different systems, devices, applications or products to connect and communicate in a coordinated way, without effort from the end user (14)." Section 4.4.2 definition (p. 42), citing IEEE Standard Computer Dictionary (1990). Alternative definition (who-iris/library/9789240093362-eng/sections/sec-023-44-design-system-architecture.md): "The ability of two or more systems or components to exchange information and to use the information that has been exchanged"
